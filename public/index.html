Race rules

  • Attendance moves you. Every approved session counts. The advisor with the most sessions leads the track, and everyone else sits behind in proportion, so positions shift up and down as the counts change.
  • Photo or it didn't happen. Every check-in needs a photo with Miguel or Frida.
  • Miguel or Frida approve every photo. A check-in counts only after one of them approves it, so AI-made or reused photos don't count.
  • Group photos count for everyone in them. Tick every advisor who's in the shot.
  • One check-in per person per day. A second photo from the same day won't count twice.
  • The milk mile. Whoever has the fewest approved sessions when the semester ends runs four laps and drinks a pint of milk before each one. A tie for last means everyone tied runs it.
